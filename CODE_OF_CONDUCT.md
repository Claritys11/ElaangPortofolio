# Code Of Conduct

## Our Standard

Contributors should keep discussion respectful, constructive, and focused on improving the project.

Examples of welcome behavior:

- Clear, kind technical feedback.
- Respect for differing experience levels.
- Good-faith questions and suggestions.
- Responsible handling of security-sensitive information.

Unwelcome behavior includes harassment, personal attacks, public disclosure of private security reports, or sharing credentials and personal data without permission.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove comments and contributions that do not meet this standard. Repeated or severe violations may result in being blocked from project spaces.
